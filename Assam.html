Top Places to Visit in Assam

Kaziranga National Park
UNESCO World Heritage Site, home to the largest population of the one-horned rhinoceros.
Kamakhya Temple
A revered Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Kamakhya, located atop Nilachal Hill in Guwahati.
Majuli Island
World's largest river island known for its monasteries, tribal culture, and scenic beauty.
Sivasagar
Historic town with temples, tanks, and remnants of the Ahom Kingdom’s architectural heritage.
Manas National Park
UNESCO-listed biosphere reserve known for its rich biodiversity and scenic landscapes.
Haflong
Assam’s only hill station, surrounded by rolling hills, lakes, and tribal communities.
Umananda Island
World’s smallest inhabited river island, with a sacred temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.
Jorhat Tea Gardens
The Tea Capital of Assam, offering lush plantations and insight into tea production history.
