Top Places to Visit in Punjab

Golden Temple
Also known as Harmandir Sahib, it is the holiest Sikh shrine located in Amritsar.
Jallianwala Bagh
Historic garden in Amritsar commemorating the 1919 massacre by British troops.
Wagah Border
Famous for the ceremonial border closing ceremony between India and Pakistan.
Rock Garden of Chandigarh
Sculpture garden built from industrial & home waste materials by Nek Chand.
Anandpur Sahib
One of the most important sacred places in Sikhism, founded by Guru Tegh Bahadur.
