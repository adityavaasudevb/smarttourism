Top Places to Visit in Dadra and Nagar Haveli

Vanganga Garden
A beautifully landscaped garden with a lake, bridges, flowers, and walking paths in Silvassa.
Satya Narayan Temple
A peaceful and well-maintained temple surrounded by scenic beauty in Silvassa.
Dudhni Lake
A serene lake offering boating, water sports, and a peaceful escape into nature.
Vasona Lion Safari
A wildlife sanctuary where visitors can spot lions in their natural habitat via safari rides.
Tribal Cultural Museum
Museum that showcases the lifestyle, tools, clothing, and culture of local tribes.
