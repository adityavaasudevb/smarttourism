Top Places to Visit in Chandigarh

Rock Garden
Unique sculpture garden created from industrial & home waste, designed by Nek Chand.
Sukhna Lake
Picturesque man-made lake perfect for boating, walking, and relaxing in nature.
Zakir Hussain Rose Garden
Asia's largest rose garden with over 1600 varieties of roses and medicinal plants.
Capitol Complex
UNESCO World Heritage Site designed by Le Corbusier, featuring modernist government buildings.
Chhatbir Zoo
Wildlife zoo located near Chandigarh housing a wide variety of animals and birds.
Elante Mall
One of the largest shopping malls in North India, offering retail, dining, and entertainment.
