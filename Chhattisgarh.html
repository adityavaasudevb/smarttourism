Top Places to Visit in Chhattisgarh

Chitrakote Falls
Also called the 'Niagara of India', this wide and scenic waterfall is located on the Indravati River.
Kanger Valley National Park
Dense forest reserve known for unique limestone caves, wildlife, and natural beauty.
Bhoramdeo Temple
An ancient temple complex often called the "Khajuraho of Chhattisgarh" for its detailed carvings.
Tirathgarh Falls
A spectacular waterfall in Bastar surrounded by lush greenery and natural tranquility.
Maitri Bagh Zoo
A zoo and park built as a symbol of Indo-Soviet friendship, featuring boating and gardens.
Danteshwari Temple
One of the 52 Shakti Peethas, this temple is revered by the tribal people of Bastar region.
