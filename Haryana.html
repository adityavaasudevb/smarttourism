Top Places to Visit in Haryana

Brahma Sarovar, Kurukshetra
A sacred water tank in Kurukshetra associated with Lord Brahma and the Mahabharata.
Sultanpur National Park
Famous bird sanctuary near Gurgaon, ideal for nature lovers and bird watchers.
Sheetla Mata Mandir
A revered Hindu temple dedicated to the goddess Sheetla Devi in Gurgaon.
Pinjore Gardens
Mughal-style garden near Panchkula, known for fountains, greenery, and heritage structures.
Morni Hills
The only hill station in Haryana, offering scenic beauty, trekking, and serene lakes.
