Top Places to Visit in Madhya Pradesh

Khajuraho Temples
World-famous UNESCO site known for stunning sculptures and intricate erotic carvings.
Dhuandhar Falls
Spectacular waterfall in Bhedaghat with smoky mist and marble rock gorges.
Bandhavgarh National Park
Famous tiger reserve and one of the best places in India to spot a Royal Bengal Tiger.
Sanchi Stupa
Ancient Buddhist monument and UNESCO World Heritage site dating back to 3rd century BCE.
Gwalior Fort
Majestic hilltop fort with palaces, temples, and panoramic views of Gwalior city.
