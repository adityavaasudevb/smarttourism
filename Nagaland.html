Top Places to Visit in Nagaland

Kohima
Nagaland’s capital, famous for WWII cemetery, local culture, and the Hornbill Festival.
Dzukou Valley
Stunning valley on the Nagaland-Manipur border known for trekking and seasonal flowers.
Touphema Village
Cultural village offering traditional Naga hospitality, food, and cottages.
Mokokchung
A cultural hub of the Ao Naga tribe with scenic views and warm locals.
Longwa Village
Unique village where the Indo-Myanmar border runs through the Angh’s house.
