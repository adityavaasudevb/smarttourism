Top Places to Visit in Arunachal Pradesh

Tawang Monastery
India’s largest monastery located in the beautiful Tawang valley, showcasing Buddhist heritage.
Sela Pass
Scenic high-altitude mountain pass offering snowy vistas and the tranquil Sela Lake.
Bomdila Monastery
A peaceful Buddhist monastery in Bomdila surrounded by panoramic Himalayan views.
Ziro Valley
Lush green valley known for rice fields, tribal villages, and the popular Ziro Music Festival.
Ita Fort
Historical fort in Itanagar built in the 14th–15th century, from which the city derives its name.
Namdapha National Park
A biodiversity hotspot in the Eastern Himalayas with diverse flora, fauna, and dense forest trails.
Sangti Valley
Hidden gem near Dirang famous for scenic beauty, migratory black-necked cranes, and peaceful vibes.
Mechuka Valley
Remote valley in West Siang district known for natural beauty, tribal culture, and a 400-year-old monastery.
