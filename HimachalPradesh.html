Top Places to Visit in Himachal Pradesh

Manali
Popular hill station offering snow-capped mountains, adventure sports, and scenic beauty along the Beas River.
Shimla
The capital city known for colonial architecture, Mall Road, and panoramic mountain views.
Kasol
A serene hamlet in Parvati Valley, loved by backpackers for its scenic trails and vibrant culture.
Dharamshala
Spiritual home of the Dalai Lama with Tibetan monasteries and picturesque landscapes.
McLeod Ganj
Charming hill town known for its Tibetan culture, Dal Lake, and tranquil monasteries.
