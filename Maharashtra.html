Top Places to Visit in Maharashtra

Gateway of India
An iconic arch monument in Mumbai, symbolizing the grandeur of colonial architecture.
Ajanta Caves
Ancient rock-cut Buddhist cave monuments renowned for their art and architecture.
Raigad Fort
The historic fort of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj offering panoramic views and rich heritage.
Elephanta Caves
UNESCO World Heritage Site with rock-cut sculptures dedicated to Lord Shiva.
Lonavala
Popular hill station famous for waterfalls, forts, caves, and monsoon greenery.
