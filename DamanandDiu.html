Top Places to Visit in Daman and Diu

Moti Daman Fort
A 16th-century Portuguese fort with walls enclosing government buildings and a church.
Jampore Beach
A serene beach perfect for long walks, horse rides, and peaceful sunsets.
St. Paul's Church
One of the best examples of Baroque architecture in India located in Diu.
Diu Fort
Massive Portuguese fort with beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.
Ghoghla Beach
Clean, quiet beach offering parasailing and other water sports.
Daman Lighthouse
A tall lighthouse in Daman giving panoramic views of the sea and coast.
