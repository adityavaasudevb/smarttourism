Top Places to Visit in Manipur

Loktak Lake
Famous for its floating phumdis and home to the only floating national park in the world.
Kangla Fort
Historic fort in Imphal with ancient temples and a deep cultural legacy of Manipur.
Ima Keithel
Asia’s largest all-women run market, offering traditional goods and handicrafts.
Sendra Island
A scenic island resort on Loktak Lake offering panoramic views and serene retreats.
Shaheed Minar
A monument honoring the brave martyrs who fought against British colonial rule.
