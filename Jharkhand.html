Top Places to Visit in Jharkhand

Hundru Falls
One of the highest waterfalls in Jharkhand, surrounded by scenic forests and rocks.
Baidyanath Temple, Deoghar
One of the twelve Jyotirlingas and a major pilgrimage site for Hindus.
Ranchi Lake
A serene lake in the heart of Ranchi, perfect for relaxation and boating.
Birsa Biological Park
A well-maintained zoo and botanical garden near Ranchi, home to many species.
Netarhat
Known as the 'Queen of Chotanagpur', famous for beautiful sunrises and sunsets.
