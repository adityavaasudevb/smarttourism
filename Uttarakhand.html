Top Places to Visit in Uttarakhand

Kedarnath Temple
Sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, nestled in the Himalayas.
Mussoorie
Charming hill station known as the Queen of Hills with breathtaking views and colonial charm.
Rishikesh
Spiritual town by the Ganges, famous for yoga, temples, and adventure sports.
Nainital
Beautiful lake town surrounded by hills, ideal for boating and scenic walks.
Valley of Flowers
UNESCO World Heritage Site with vibrant alpine flora in a picturesque Himalayan setting.
