Top Places to Visit in Gujarat

Statue of Unity
World’s tallest statue, dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, located near the Sardar Sarovar Dam.
Somnath Temple
One of the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines of Shiva, known for its spiritual significance and seaside location.
Rann of Kutch
A white salt desert known for the Rann Utsav, cultural performances, and camel rides under moonlight.
Gir National Park
Home to the majestic Asiatic lions, this park offers thrilling wildlife safaris and diverse flora and fauna.
Saputara
The only hill station in Gujarat, perfect for nature lovers and trekkers looking for a cool retreat.
