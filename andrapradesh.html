Top Places to Visit in Andhra Pradesh

Tirupati
Famous pilgrimage city home to the revered Sri Venkateswara Temple atop the Tirumala hills.
Undavalli Caves
Ancient rock-cut cave temples near Vijayawada showcasing stunning monolithic architecture.
RK Beach, Visakhapatnam
Popular beach in Vizag known for its scenic views, serene atmosphere, and Submarine Museum.
Araku Valley
Picturesque hill station in Eastern Ghats famous for coffee plantations and tribal culture.
Kailasagiri
Hilltop park in Visakhapatnam with panoramic views, ropeway, and giant Shiva-Parvati statues.
Gandikota
Known as the 'Grand Canyon of India', Gandikota features a fort and a stunning gorge on Pennar River.
Amaravati Stupa
An ancient Buddhist site with a large stupa dating back to the 3rd century BCE.
Borra Caves
Spectacular limestone caves in the Ananthagiri Hills, known for stalactites and stalagmites formations.
