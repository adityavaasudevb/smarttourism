Top Places to Visit in Goa

Basilica of Bom Jesus
UNESCO World Heritage church famous for holding the mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier.
Calangute Beach
Popular beach offering water sports, shacks, nightlife, and a vibrant vibe.
Fort Aguada
17th-century Portuguese fort with a lighthouse offering stunning sea views.
Dudhsagar Falls
Spectacular four-tiered waterfall located on the Goa-Karnataka border, best during monsoon.
Chapora Fort
Historic fort offering panoramic views of Vagator Beach and the Arabian Sea.
