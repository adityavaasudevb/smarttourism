Top Places to Visit in Jammu and Kashmir

Dal Lake
Famous for its beautiful shikaras and houseboats, Dal Lake is the heart of Srinagar.
Gulmarg
A popular skiing and adventure destination, surrounded by snow-capped mountains.
Vaishno Devi
A revered Hindu pilgrimage site nestled in the Trikuta Mountains near Katra.
Sonamarg
Known as the 'Meadow of Gold', it's a gateway to high-altitude Himalayan trekking routes.
Pahalgam
Scenic town in the Anantnag district, popular for the Amarnath Yatra and lush valleys.
