Top Places to Visit in Tripura

Ujjayanta Palace
A beautiful palace in Agartala, now a state museum showcasing Tripura's royal history and culture.
Neermahal
A scenic water palace located in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake, reflecting Mughal and Hindu architecture.
Unakoti
An ancient Shaivite pilgrimage site featuring giant rock-cut sculptures and bas-reliefs in a forested hill.
Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary
Home to rare species like clouded leopards, this sanctuary also has boating and botanical gardens.
Chabimura
Famous for its rock carvings on the steep mountain walls along the Gomati River and boat rides.
