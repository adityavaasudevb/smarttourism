Top Places to Visit in Puducherry

Rock Beach
Scenic beach with rocky shoreline, a great spot for sunrise and sea breeze in Puducherry.
Auroville
Experimental township promoting unity and peace, home to the iconic Matrimandir.
Sacred Heart Basilica
A stunning neo-gothic Catholic church known for its stained glass and architecture.
Sri Aurobindo Ashram
Spiritual community and serene retreat established by Sri Aurobindo and The Mother.
French Quarter
Charming neighborhood with colonial architecture, colorful streets, and French cafes.
