Top Places to Visit in Rajasthan

Hawa Mahal
Iconic "Palace of Winds" in Jaipur, known for its intricate pink sandstone façade and lattice windows.
Mehrangarh Fort
A massive fort in Jodhpur perched on a rocky hill, offering panoramic views and rich history.
Jaisalmer Fort
Also known as the "Golden Fort", it is a UNESCO World Heritage site made of yellow sandstone.
Amber Fort
A majestic fort near Jaipur with stunning palaces, courtyards, and mirror work halls.
