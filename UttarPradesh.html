Top Places to Visit in Uttar Pradesh

Taj Mahal
One of the Seven Wonders of the World, this marble mausoleum is a symbol of eternal love in Agra.
Varanasi Ghats
Spiritual heart of India, famous for Ganga Aarti, rituals, and the sacred River Ganges.
Fatehpur Sikri
UNESCO World Heritage site and former Mughal capital with stunning red sandstone architecture.
Bara Imambara
A grand Mughal-style monument in Lucknow known for its central hall and Bhool Bhulaiya maze.
Sarnath
Sacred Buddhist site where Lord Buddha gave his first sermon, featuring stupas and museums.
