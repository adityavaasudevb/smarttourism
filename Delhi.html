Top Places to Visit in Delhi

India Gate
Iconic war memorial built in honor of Indian soldiers who died in World War I.
Red Fort
A UNESCO World Heritage Site, symbol of India's rich Mughal architecture.
Humayun’s Tomb
The tomb of Mughal Emperor Humayun, an early example of Mughal architecture in India.
Lotus Temple
Bahá’í House of Worship known for its flower-like shape and peaceful ambiance.
Qutub Minar
World's tallest brick minaret built in 1193, part of the Qutub complex.
Akshardham Temple
Grand Hindu temple complex showcasing Indian culture, spirituality, and architecture.
