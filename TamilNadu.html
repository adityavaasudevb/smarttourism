Top Places to Visit in Tamil Nadu

Brihadeeswarar Temple
A UNESCO World Heritage Site in Thanjavur, showcasing Dravidian architecture and Chola grandeur.
Meenakshi Temple
An iconic temple in Madurai known for its towering gopurams and intricate carvings.
Ooty
The Queen of Hill Stations, Ooty offers lush tea gardens, scenic views, and a famous toy train.
Kanyakumari
Southernmost tip of India where you can witness the confluence of three oceans and beautiful sunsets.
Mahabalipuram
Famous for its rock-cut temples and sculptures, it's a UNESCO World Heritage site on the Coromandel Coast.
