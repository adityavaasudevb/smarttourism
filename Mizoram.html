Top Places to Visit in Mizoram

Aizawl
The picturesque capital city offering panoramic views, vibrant culture, and local markets.
Vantawng Falls
Mizoram's highest waterfall cascading amidst dense forests near Thenzawl.
Reiek
A scenic mountain village offering views of the Mizo Hills and cultural experiences.
Tam Dil Lake
A serene natural lake near Aizawl, perfect for boating and surrounded by forests.
Murlen National Park
Rich biodiversity hotspot with semi-evergreen forests and rare wildlife species.
