Top Places to Visit in Bihar

Mahabodhi Temple
UNESCO World Heritage Site in Bodh Gaya where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment.
Nalanda University
Ancient learning center and one of the world’s oldest universities.
Vishwa Shanti Stupa
Peace Pagoda in Rajgir on a scenic hilltop, symbolizing world peace.
Golghar
Historic granary in Patna with a panoramic view of the city and river Ganges.
Vikramshila University
Ancient university established in the 8th century by King Dharmapala.
Barabar Caves
Oldest surviving rock-cut caves in India dating back to the Mauryan Empire.
