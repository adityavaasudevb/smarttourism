Top Places to Visit in West Bengal

Victoria Memorial
An iconic marble museum in Kolkata symbolizing the grandeur of the British Raj.
Darjeeling
Famous hill station with tea gardens, toy train, and panoramic Himalayan views.
Sundarbans
UNESCO-listed mangrove forest and home to the Royal Bengal Tiger.
Dakshineswar Kali Temple
Famous riverside temple dedicated to Goddess Kali near Kolkata.
Howrah Bridge
Iconic cantilever bridge over the Hooghly River, a symbol of Kolkata.
