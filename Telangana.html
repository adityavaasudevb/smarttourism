Top Places to Visit in Telangana

Charminar
Iconic 16th-century mosque with four grand arches and minarets at the heart of Hyderabad.
Golconda Fort
Historic fortress known for its acoustics, scenic views, and legacy of the Qutb Shahi dynasty.
Ramoji Film City
World's largest film studio complex with tours, attractions, and entertainment.
Hussain Sagar Lake
Man-made lake with a giant Buddha statue, popular for boating and evening strolls.
Chowmahalla Palace
18th-century palace complex showcasing the royal grandeur of the Nizams.
Salar Jung Museum
Vast museum with rare collections of art, antiques, and historic manuscripts.
Warangal Fort
Ruins of a grand Kakatiya fort with iconic stone gateways and ancient carvings.
Kuntala Waterfall
Telangana's tallest waterfall surrounded by forested landscapes and nature trails.
