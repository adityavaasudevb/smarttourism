Top Places to Visit in Karnataka

Hampi
UNESCO World Heritage site with stunning ruins from the Vijayanagara Empire.
Mysore Palace
A majestic Indo-Saracenic palace known for its grandeur and Dussehra celebrations.
Coorg
Also known as Kodagu, a scenic hill station famous for coffee plantations and misty hills.
Gokarna
A peaceful beach town with serene shorelines and revered temples.
Jog Falls
One of India's highest waterfalls, offering breathtaking views especially during monsoons.
