Top Places to Visit in Odisha

Konark Sun Temple
A 13th-century architectural marvel shaped like a chariot dedicated to the Sun God.
Puri Jagannath Temple
One of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites and famous for the annual Rath Yatra.
Chilika Lake
Asia's largest brackish water lagoon, home to migratory birds and dolphins.
Hirakud Dam
One of the longest earthen dams in the world, built across the Mahanadi River.
Udayagiri & Khandagiri Caves
Ancient Jain rock-cut caves with inscriptions, sculptures, and religious importance.
