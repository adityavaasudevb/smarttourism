Top Places to Visit in Meghalaya

Umiam Lake
A scenic man-made reservoir near Shillong, known for water sports and picturesque views.
Nohkalikai Falls
India's tallest plunge waterfall located near Cherrapunji, surrounded by lush greenery.
Living Root Bridges
Ingenious bio-engineered bridges made from living tree roots in the rainforests of Meghalaya.
Mawsynram
The wettest place on Earth, famous for its rainfall, caves, and misty landscapes.
Dawki River
Crystal-clear river offering boating experiences with views of the riverbed below.
