Top Places to Visit in Sikkim

Tsomgo Lake
A glacial lake situated at 12,400 ft, known for its stunning reflections and sacred importance.
Rumtek Monastery
A major center of Tibetan Buddhism with exquisite architecture and peaceful surroundings.
Nathula Pass
Historic mountain pass on the Indo-China border, offering dramatic Himalayan views.
Yumthang Valley
Known as the “Valley of Flowers”, it boasts lush meadows and vibrant blooms in spring.
Zero Point
High-altitude snowy region near the Indo-China border, perfect for snow lovers and adventurers.
