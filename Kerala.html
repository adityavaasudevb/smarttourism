Top Places to Visit in Kerala

Alleppey Backwaters
Famous for houseboat cruises through scenic backwaters and paddy fields.
Munnar
A picturesque hill station known for tea gardens, misty hills, and cool climate.
Kovalam Beach
A crescent-shaped beach known for its calm waters and vibrant beachfront.
Athirappilly Falls
The 'Niagara of India', a spectacular waterfall surrounded by lush greenery.
Padmanabhaswamy Temple
A grand and sacred temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, located in Thiruvananthapuram.
